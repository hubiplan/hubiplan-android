The companion app for HUBIplan, the free club management software. It connects to your club's own HUBIplan (QR code from the member area in the browser) and brings club life to your phone and tablet:

• Start: your next event and what you still need to do
• Events by month, accept or decline with one tap, filter for open responses, reminders in your device calendar
• Registration with participation fee, tickets with QR code, waiting list
• News: messages and circulars from your club, pinboard with photos, surveys
• Instant notifications via a UnifiedPush app such as ntfy (without Google, end-to-end encrypted) or by polling
• Me: membership card (also offline), your own data, invoices and direct debit mandate, meetings, documents, training with QR check-in and shooting log, group orders
• For the board: administration as a workspace with "New", "To do" and search; membership applications, bookings, running meetings, admission with ticket scanner
• Video room link on events; share calendar invitations (.ics) to create an event
• Your club's colours and crest, light or dark mode, layouts for phone and tablet
• Several clubs in one app, German or English

The app only talks to your club's HUBIplan. It contains no ads, no analytics and no Google services (no Firebase, no Play services). Screenshots are blocked by default to protect personal data; you can allow them under "My account". The app requires that your club uses HUBIplan and has given you access to the member area.
